Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Who this covers

This policy applies to learngerman.cc ("the site"), operated by Bharath Kumar, contactable at shettybharath4@gmail.com. See also our Impressum for full legal contact details.

What we collect, and why

If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive and store your Google account ID, email address, and display name, plus timestamps of when your account was created and when you last signed in. This is used solely to show that you're signed in and let you manage your account (sign out, delete your account). No feature of this site currently depends on this data beyond that — there is no analytics tie-in, no marketing use, and no sharing beyond the sub-processors listed below. If you never sign in, none of this is collected.

Legal basis

We process this data based on your consent: signing in with Google is entirely optional and requires an explicit click on your part. Nothing is sent to Google or stored about you until you do this.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account yourself (via the account menu → "Delete account") or ask us to via the contact above. Because sign-in sessions are self-contained (not looked up from our database on every visit), a device that was already signed in may continue showing your name for up to 7 days after you delete your account elsewhere, until that session naturally expires. Operational and access logs kept by our infrastructure provider (Cloudflare) may also persist briefly beyond a deletion, per their own standard retention practices.

Who else sees this data

  • Google — acts as the identity provider for sign-in. Google's own privacy policy governs whatever Google itself collects during that process, separately from what we store.
  • Cloudflare — hosts this site and stores account data in its D1 database (configured to keep the data in the EU). Cloudflare is headquartered in the United States and relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses as its legal basis for any data transfer this may involve.

We do not sell or share your data with anyone else.
