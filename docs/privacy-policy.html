Privacy Policy for Scorer

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Scorer is a badminton and table tennis match score tracker for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data We Collect

None. Scorer does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or analytics of any kind.

How Your Data Is Stored

All information you enter — team names, team colors, and match scores — is stored only on your own device, using Apple's standard on-device storage (UserDefaults). It is never sent to us or to any third party, because the app makes no network requests at all.

Third-Party Services

Scorer does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. It has no login, no account creation, and no internet connectivity requirement.

Children's Privacy

Because Scorer collects no data from any user, it does not collect data from children either.

Deleting Your Data

All app data lives only on your device. Deleting the app removes all associated data immediately.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds a feature that uses data differently), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
