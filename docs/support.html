Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a match?

From the home screen, tap Start Match, choose a sport (Badminton or Table Tennis), pick or add two teams, then tap Start Match again.

How do I correct a score?

While a match is running, use the Undo button under either team's score to remove their most recent point.

How do I end a match early?

Tap the close button in the top corner of the score screen, then choose which team won — or end the match without declaring a winner.

Where is my data stored?

Everything — teams, colors, and match history — stays on your device. Scorer has no account, no sign-in, and no internet connection requirement. See our Privacy Policy for details.
